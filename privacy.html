Privacy Policy

MeatMode: Carnivore Diet Tracker · Last updated November 20, 2026
The short version: your health data never leaves your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, no data sales. Purchases are handled by Apple; an anonymous purchase record (no name, email or health data) is kept by our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

What we collect

  • Onboarding answers (goals, symptoms, experience, fasting preference) are stored only on your device and used to personalize your plan.
  • Food logs, fasting sessions, challenge progress, weight entries and journal photos are stored on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Subscription records. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to keep a record of free trials, subscriptions, renewals and restores. RevenueCat receives the App Store transaction (product, price, currency, dates, trial status) together with a random anonymous ID created on your device. It does not receive your name, email, health data or advertising identifier, and it is not used to track you across apps. This record lets us confirm your subscription and understand how many people start trials and subscribe. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your health data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.
  • We do not include advertising SDKs, and we do not track you. The only third-party service in the app is RevenueCat, for the anonymous subscription records described above.

Analytics & crashes

If you opt in via iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymized crash diagnostics and usage metrics with us to improve stability. Disable this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. Subscription management is handled by Apple under your Apple ID. To ask us to delete the anonymous subscription record, email us from the app's Settings → Support and include your purchase date.

Health disclaimer

MeatMode is a tracking and education tool, not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes, especially if you have a medical condition or take medication.
