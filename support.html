Fast answers

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → MeatMode. You can switch plans or cancel there. Canceling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the paid period ends. You can also tap "Manage subscription" inside MeatMode's Settings sheet.

I paid but MeatMode still shows the paywall.

Open MeatMode → paywall → Restore (top right), or Settings sheet → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase. Still stuck? Email us with your order ID from the Apple receipt.

How does the 30-day challenge work?

Day 1 starts the moment you unlock MeatMode. Every day you stay strict keeps your streak alive. Milestones land at days 3, 7, 14, 21 and 30 — each with a note on what's typically changing in your body. You can restart the challenge anytime from the Settings sheet.

Which fasting protocols are supported?

16:8, 18:6 and OMAD (23:1). Pick one during onboarding or change it later in Settings. The timer ring and eating window adapt automatically.

Where is my data stored? Can I export it?

Everything — food logs, symptoms, weights, photos — lives on your device only. No account, no cloud. Deleting the app deletes the data. Progress share cards can be exported from the in-app feed, and your shopping list can be shared as text.

Is MeatMode medical advice?

No. MeatMode is a tracking and education tool. Talk to a qualified professional before major dietary changes — especially if you take medication, are pregnant or nursing, or manage a medical condition.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Both plans (Weekly $9.99/week and Yearly $89.99/year) start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. You won't be charged during the trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends in Settings → your name → Subscriptions and you pay nothing; otherwise the plan you picked starts automatically.

Billing & refunds

All payments are processed by Apple. Refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com — Apple handles them directly, and we can't issue refunds ourselves.
